Our Vision

We believe IT support should be proactive, not reactive. StrataMind uses AI to resolve routine issues instantly, route complex tickets to the right owner, and continuously enrich a shared knowledge base—so teams stay focused on meaningful work while support becomes faster, calmer, and measurably better.

We envision a world where IT support evolves from a reactive service into a true strategic advantage.
